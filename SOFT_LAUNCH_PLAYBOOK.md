# Get Cleaning Quote — Soft Launch Playbook

## Goal

Recruit 3–5 cleaning businesses for a 30-day hands-on pilot. The purpose is to prove that businesses will share their quote page, receive real requests, and send proposals—not to maximize signups.

## Early-access offer

> Get Cleaning Quote gives your cleaning business a branded quote page, consistent starting estimates, and a simple dashboard for turning inquiries into professional proposals.

- 30-day free pilot
- No card required
- Direct setup help
- Custom pricing rules
- Customer and owner email notifications
- Proposal sending and acceptance tracking
- Pilot feedback helps determine the paid plan and roadmap

Do not promise features that are not live, including payments, scheduling, staff accounts, text messaging, or accounting integrations.

## Ideal first customer

Prioritize owner-operated residential cleaning businesses that:

- Serve one local market
- Have roughly 1–15 cleaners
- Already receive inquiries from a website, Google Business Profile, Facebook, or Instagram
- Ask customers repetitive questions before giving a price
- Currently manage leads through calls, texts, email, spreadsheets, or paper
- Have a visible owner or manager who can make a quick decision

Avoid franchises, national companies, businesses without current contact information, and companies already using a sophisticated booking platform during the first pilot.

## Qualification checklist

Score one point for each:

1. Active website or social profile
2. Residential cleaning is a primary service
3. Public email, contact form, or business phone
4. No clear online quote workflow
5. Recent reviews or posts indicate the business is active

Contact prospects scoring 4–5 first.

I am inviting a small group of cleaning businesses to use it free for 30 days in exchange for honest feedback. I will help set up your quote page and pricing rules personally.

{{ your_name }}
getcleaningquote.co

## Short social message

Hi {{ first_name }} — I built a simple quote-request and proposal tool for independent cleaning businesses. I am onboarding a few companies free for 30 days and helping with setup personally. Would a 15-minute look be useful for {{ business_name }}?

## Follow-up sequence

- Day 0: Send the personalized initial message.
- Day 3: Follow up with one specific observation about their current quote process.
- Day 7: Send a final short note offering the 15-minute walkthrough.
- Stop after the third message unless they reply.

Follow-up example:

> Just following up, {{ first_name }}. The main idea is to replace the back-and-forth of collecting bedrooms, bathrooms, frequency, and notes with one branded link. I can configure a sample page for {{ business_name }} during the walkthrough.

## 15-minute demo

1. Ask how quote inquiries arrive today.
2. Open the business-facing customer quote page.
3. Submit a sample request.
4. Show the owner notification and dashboard.
5. Generate, edit, and send the proposal.
6. Show customer acceptance and the updated status.
7. Ask what would prevent them from using it with a real customer this week.

Keep the demo focused on their workflow. Do not lead with a feature tour.

## Pilot onboarding

1. Business creates and verifies its account.
2. Confirm business name and owner email.
3. Configure starting prices, room adjustments, and recurring discounts.
4. Save and test the public quote link.
5. Have the owner submit, send, and accept one test proposal.
6. Give the owner their shareable `/q/<business-slug>` link.
7. Ask them to use the link with at least three real prospects.
8. Schedule feedback after seven days.

## Feedback questions

- How were quote requests handled before this pilot?
- Did you share the link with real customers? Why or why not?
- Which details were missing from the request form?
- Did the estimate match how you normally price?
- Did the proposal save time?
- What would make this worth paying for monthly?
- What is the one feature you expected but could not find?

## Prospect tracker columns

Use a spreadsheet with:

- Business name
- Owner/contact name
- City/service area
- Website
- Email
- Phone/social profile
- Qualification score
- Current quote method
- First contact date
- Follow-up date
- Status
- Notes

Suggested statuses: `Researching`, `Ready to contact`, `Contacted`, `Replied`, `Demo booked`, `Pilot active`, `Not now`, `Not a fit`.

## Pilot success criteria

The soft launch is successful when:

- 3 businesses finish onboarding
- At least 2 share their link with real customers
- At least 10 real quote requests are submitted
- At least 3 proposals are sent
- At least 1 business says it would pay to continue

## Safety and outreach rules

- Contact only publicly listed business channels.
- Personalize each message; do not mass-send identical email.
- Identify yourself and the product accurately.
- Honor opt-outs and stop after the planned follow-ups.
- Never claim Get Cleaning Quote provides the cleaning service.
