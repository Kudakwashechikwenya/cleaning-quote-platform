Why I built it

A small product built around a real, repetitive task.

I kept seeing independent cleaning businesses ask the same questions before they could price a job: What kind of property is it? How many rooms? How often do you need service? Is there anything unusual we should know?

Get Cleaning Quote puts those answers in one place and helps the owner respond professionally. I am starting locally in DFW so I can work directly with the first businesses, watch how the tool fits their day, and improve it from honest feedback.

— Kuda
